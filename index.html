About
I build performance and technology that ask what happens to connection when it's mediated by machines. I co-led bluemouth inc. from 2020–2025 and my current research, supported by a Chalmers Arts Fellowship, is an independent practice exploring artificial intimacy through performance, installation, and interactive media. I'm open to new collaborations, commissions, and conversations.
